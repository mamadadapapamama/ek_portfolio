About

I'm EK, a Korean product designer based in Singapore. I've designed email for Comcast customers in the American Midwest, Autodesk's cloud developer platform with teams across the US and Tel Aviv, AI product discovery for retailers at ViSenze, and banking and telco apps for Southeast Asia.

Since 2024 I've also built my own AI products end to end — designing, coding with AI tools, launching and iterating with real families. It changed how I work: I prototype in code, and I know what it takes to ship. I'm now looking for a Staff role setting design direction for products used across markets.
