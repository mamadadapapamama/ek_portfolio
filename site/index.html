How I work

Principles
01

Find the real problem

I start where the team is least sure. Research, data and stakeholder conversations until we agree on what we're actually solving — and what success means for the business.

02

Align people, not just pixels

Most stalled projects are alignment problems. I run workshops, make trade-offs visible, and turn ambiguity into a direction product, engineering and leadership can commit to.

03

Design for who isn't in the room

Seniors in the US Midwest, merchandisers on five commerce platforms, non-native parents in Singapore. I test early with the people furthest from the team — and design for their context, not ours.

04

Prototype in code, then learn

I use AI tools to prototype and ship real software, launch small, and let real usage shape v2. A v1 is a hypothesis — I put it in front of people fast.
