The project

Designing in three dimensions.

A reminder that design isn't only screens. Taking a tired space and reworking it — light, flow, materials, proportion — uses the same muscles as shaping a product: a clear vision, then patient, hands-on craft.

I planned the layout, chose the palette and materials, and did much of the work myself — learning by building, the same way I ship software.

The process
  1. 01Surveyed the space and set a clear direction for light, flow, and feel.
  2. 02Planned the layout and built a palette of materials, colours, and finishes.
  3. 03Did the hands-on work — demolition, surfaces, fittings — step by step.
  4. 04Styled and finished the rooms down to the last detail.
