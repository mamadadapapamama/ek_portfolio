The idea

Turn any book into a tutor.

Language learners get stuck between dry textbook drills and real content that's just out of reach. Pikabook closes that gap: point it at a book and it becomes an interactive tutor — explaining, quizzing, and adapting to your level as you read.

Under Noworrytingxie, I owned the product from concept to a shipped iOS app — defining the experience, designing the interface, and building it with an AI-assisted, LLM-powered stack.

What I did
  1. 01Defined the MVP and wrote the product spec — scoping what a v1 tutor needed to do well.
  2. 02Designed the full interface and reading-to-learning flow in Figma.
  3. 03Built the app with an AI-assisted workflow in Cursor, wiring Firebase for auth & data.
  4. 04Integrated Gemini and Google Cloud APIs for the tutoring intelligence.
  5. 05Shipped to the App Store and set up the web presence on GitHub Pages.
