The brief

A site the teachers can own.

The kindergarten needed a public face that felt as gentle and considered as its philosophy — and, crucially, one the teachers could keep up to date on their own between classes.

I designed a calm, content-first site and paired it with a friendly CMS, so announcements, photos, and pages can be edited safely without any developer in the loop.

What I did
  1. 01Designed a calm, content-first visual language matched to the school's ethos.
  2. 02Built the site on Astro with Tailwind for a fast, lightweight result.
  3. 03Set up Decap CMS so non-technical teachers can edit content with confidence.
  4. 04Deployed on Cloudflare with a smooth publish workflow and custom domain.

Teachers publish in minutes, not tickets.
